ABOUT US

Adventure Begins In The Mountains

Kashmir Ski & Snowboarding School is a professional ski and snowboarding school based in Gulmarg, Kashmir. We offer private and group lessons for beginners, intermediate and advanced skiers and snowboarders.

With experienced local instructors and a focus on safety, technique and personalized training, we help you enjoy an unforgettable experience on the slopes of Gulmarg.
