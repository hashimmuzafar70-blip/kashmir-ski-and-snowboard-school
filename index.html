Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about skiing and snowboarding in Gulmarg.

Can beginners join?

Yes! Beginners can learn the basics with guidance suited to their experience level.

What age can children start?

Our school welcomes learners aged four and above. A parent or guardian should discuss a child's needs with us before booking.

Do you teach snowboarding?

Yes, we offer snowboarding lessons as well as skiing lessons.

What should I bring?

Wear warm winter clothing, gloves, and suitable socks. Ask us what equipment is available and what you need to arrange separately.

How do I book a lesson?

Use the WhatsApp booking button already on our website to send us your preferred date and lesson details.

Where are you located?

Kashmir Ski and Snowboarding School is based in Gulmarg, Kashmir.
